Suggested (not required) place to keep your exported data file.

From Settings (or the export icon in the header), click "Export data file". Your
browser downloads project-data-YYYY-MM-DD.json to its normal Downloads folder —
move it into this /data folder so it travels with the app when you copy the
whole project-control-center folder to a pen drive, external drive, or cloud
storage.

When you open the app on another machine, use "Import" and point it at that
file to bring your data back.
